# Quarterly Risk Review

## Purpose

The quarterly risk review summarizes program-level risk trends, major exceptions, remediation progress, and roadmap priorities.

## Risk Themes

| Theme | Management Concern | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Vulnerability backlog | Critical findings may exceed SLA | Escalate owners and track weekly |
| Cloud control gaps | Misconfiguration may increase exposure | Validate failed controls and automate checks |
| Detection coverage | Gaps may reduce visibility | Prioritize high-risk ATT&CK techniques |
| DevSecOps adoption | Uneven controls across repositories | Expand policy gates to priority repos |
| AI memory exposure | Emerging data leakage risk | Define safeguards and review architecture |

## Decisions Needed

- Confirm risk appetite for overdue high-severity findings.
- Approve roadmap priorities for the next quarter.
- Decide whether any open exceptions require executive approval.
- Confirm resourcing for remediation and detection engineering work.
