# Incident Response Executive Summary

## Purpose

This template converts technical incident evidence into a concise management record. Complete it after containment and update it when material facts change.

## Incident Snapshot

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Incident ID | Assign from the case-management system |
| Title | Short description of the event |
| Severity | Critical / High / Medium / Low |
| Status | Investigating / Contained / Recovering / Closed |
| Detection time | Include time zone |
| Containment time | Include time zone |
| Incident commander | Named accountable lead |
| Affected services | Systems or business processes |

## Executive Narrative

State what happened, how it was detected, the current business impact, what has been contained, and what leadership needs to know. Separate confirmed facts from working hypotheses.

## Impact Assessment

| Area | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Confidentiality | Data accessed or exposed, if confirmed |
| Integrity | Unauthorized changes or trust impact |
| Availability | Downtime or degraded service |
| Users/customers | Population and material effect |
| Regulatory/contractual | Notification or reporting considerations |

## Response Summary

1. Detection and triage actions.
2. Containment performed.
3. Evidence preserved.
4. Eradication and recovery completed or planned.
5. Monitoring in place for recurrence.

## Decisions and Open Actions

| Action or decision | Owner | Due date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirm scope and affected assets | Incident Commander | Case-specific | Open |
| Validate credential and endpoint containment | Security Operations | Case-specific | Open |
| Complete root-cause analysis | Security Engineering | Case-specific | Open |
| Track corrective actions | Control owners | Case-specific | Open |

## Communications

Record internal stakeholders, legal/privacy consultation, customer or regulator notification decisions, and the approved message owner. Do not include speculation or unnecessary sensitive detail.

## Closure Statement

Close only after containment is verified, recovery is stable, evidence is retained, required notifications are completed, corrective actions have owners, and lessons learned are scheduled.
