# Exception Process

## Purpose

Security exceptions document cases where a finding, failed control, or required remediation cannot be completed within the expected timeline.

## Required Information

- Exception title
- Affected system or repository
- Associated risk ID
- Business justification
- Compensating controls
- Requested expiration date
- Owner
- Approver
- Review cadence

## Workflow

1. Owner submits an exception request before the SLA deadline.
2. Security reviews severity, exploitability, business impact, and compensating controls.
3. Executive sponsor or delegated risk owner approves or rejects the request.
4. Approved exceptions are tracked in the risk register.
5. Expired exceptions are reviewed and either remediated, renewed, or escalated.

## Decision Criteria

- Is the risk exploitable?
- Is the affected asset business critical?
- Are compensating controls active and validated?
- Is there a clear expiration date?
- Is leadership aware of the residual risk?
