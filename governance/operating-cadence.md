# Operating Cadence

| Cadence | Meeting | Purpose | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Security operations review | Review alerts, open incidents, and triage bottlenecks | Updated triage actions |
| Weekly | Vulnerability remediation review | Review critical and high findings approaching SLA | Updated remediation backlog |
| Biweekly | Cloud and DevSecOps review | Review control failures, pipeline gates, and exceptions | Risk decisions and owner actions |
| Monthly | Security metrics review | Review KPIs, trends, and overdue risks | Monthly scorecard |
| Monthly | Executive security briefing | Summarize posture, risk, wins, and priorities | Monthly security report |
| Quarterly | Risk review | Review risk appetite, exceptions, roadmap, and program maturity | Quarterly risk review |
