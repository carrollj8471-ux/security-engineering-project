# Remediation SLA Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines expected remediation timelines for security findings tracked by the security engineering program.

## SLA Targets

| Severity | Target Remediation Time | Escalation |
|---|---:|---|
| Critical | 15 days | Security lead and executive sponsor |
| High | 30 days | Security lead and system owner manager |
| Medium | 60 days | Security lead and owner |
| Low | 90 days | Owner review |

## SLA Rules

- Internet-facing and actively exploited findings may be escalated above their base severity.
- SLA countdown starts when the finding is assigned to an owner.
- Closure requires evidence of remediation or an approved exception.
- Overdue critical and high findings are included in executive reporting.

## Exception Handling

If an owner cannot remediate within SLA, they must submit a risk acceptance request before the due date.
